# Kodiak Robotics

Kodiak Robotics, now operating publicly as Kodiak AI, is an autonomous vehicle company founded in 2018 by CEO Don Burnette, headquartered in California with a Texas operations center. Its flagship product is the Kodiak Driver — an AI autonomy system pairing onboard intelligence with modular, swappable sensor hardware and offboard services — sold into long-haul trucking, defense, and industrial ground transportation.
