Textron Systems and Kodiak AI have integrated the Kodiak Driver into the RIPSAW M3, creating a rugged, autonomous, uncrewed military ground vehicle. This collaboration aims to enhance military automation and explore future opportunities with the U.S. Department of Defense and allied nations.
