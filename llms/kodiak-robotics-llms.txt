# Kodiak AI

> Kodiak AI, formerly known as Kodiak Robotics, is developing self-driving technology for freight trucks. The company focuses on autonomous trucking solutions for long-haul freight transportation.

## Company Information

Kodiak is pioneering autonomous trucking technology to make roads safer and freight transportation more efficient. The company operates autonomous semi-trucks on freight routes.
